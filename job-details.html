Software Engineer – Laravel & Vue JS

Vacancy
3
Job Responsibilities
Our ideal candidate exhibits deep proficiency in modern UI frameworks—with a strong affinity for Angular and Material Design—to craft dynamic user interfaces.
 You will excel in architecting scalable Single Page Applications (SPAs) and integrating robust RESTful APIs to power seamless user experiences.
 A proven track record with Angular modules or developing custom web components is essential, along with hands-on experience in Node.js, TypeScript, and webpack.
 You must ensure cross-browser efficiency while minimizing bugs and delivering pixel-perfect, mobile-first designs.
 Expertise in CSS preprocessors, media queries, and image optimization, paired with rigorous debugging skills, is vital.
Employment Status
Full-time
Educational Requirements
Candidates should hold a relevant degree in Computer Science or a related field, ensuring a solid foundation in modern web technologies.
 Practical training in UI/UX design and proficiency with Angular, Vue, or similar frameworks is highly preferred.
 Hands-on experience with SPA development and RESTful API integration is a must.
Experience Requirements
A minimum of 3–5 years’ experience in frontend development is required, demonstrating a mastery of modern frameworks and tools.
 Experience in developing SPAs with integrated REST APIs and building reusable modular components is essential.
 Familiarity with CSS preprocessors, media query implementation, and effective debugging techniques rounds out your expertise.
Additional Requirements
A comprehensive understanding of cross-browser optimization and proactive bug prevention is expected.
 Delivering pixel-perfect, mobile-first design conversions is a priority in this role.
 Advanced skills in CSS preprocessors, media queries, and image optimization, combined with strong debugging acumen, are required.
Job Location
Remote Opportunity
Salary
\$2000
Compensation & Other Benefits
A competitive remuneration package awaits you, featuring a robust base salary, performance bonuses, and a suite of comprehensive benefits.
 Enjoy comprehensive health coverage, paid leave, and flexible work arrangements designed to recognize your expertise and commitment.
 Our benefits also include ongoing professional development opportunities and specialized technical training.
